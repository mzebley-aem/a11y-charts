Behind the percentages

Report terms
IDEA
Individuals with Disabilities Education Act.
Part B
The part of IDEA that supports special education for eligible children and youth ages 3–21.
IEP
Individualized education program: a written plan for a child’s special education and related services.
FFY
Federal fiscal year, the reporting period used in these examples.
Percentage point
The difference between two percentages. A change from 10% to 8% is a decrease of 2 percentage points.
Source & data notes

Reference examples: Ind2.pdf, Ind5.pdf, and Ind6.pdf, supplied for this prototype. Values are transcribed from those examples and have not been independently validated.

    Only explicitly printed historical values are included. Planned targets for 2024–2025 are mentioned in the PDFs, but their exact values are not visible; they are omitted here. Missing data is never treated as zero.
